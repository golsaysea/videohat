Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

VideoHat is a web tool for creating short-form video projects, templates, captions, and export queues. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We may store project data that you choose to save, such as template settings, caption text, task names, export options, and references to selected media files.

When you connect Google Drive, VideoHat requests access only to files you select through the app. We use Google Drive file IDs, names, metadata, and temporary access tokens to let you preview or use selected media in your project.

Media Files

Local files selected in your browser are processed on your device unless you explicitly choose to upload them. Google Drive files remain in your Google account and are downloaded only when you choose to use them in VideoHat.

How We Use Information

We use your information to load projects, apply templates, preview media, generate export tasks, and provide the features you request.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your Google Drive files with other users. Project sharing or cloud export features only use data that you intentionally save or submit.

Data Storage

Some project settings may be stored locally in your browser. Cloud projects and official templates may be stored using Cloudflare services. Google Drive media stays under your Google account unless you separately upload media to another service.

Google API Data

VideoHat's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your Choices

You can remove local browser data by clearing site data in your browser. You can revoke Google Drive access from your Google Account permissions page at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the VideoHat administrator or project owner.
